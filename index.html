Worker & Volunteer Code of Conduct

1

Arrive 30 minutes before your appointed duty, sign the attendance register, and notify your Department Head at least 2 hours in advance if delayed.

2

Treat members, guests, and fellow GEMYFELL @20 volunteers with patience and grace. Avoid public arguments, divisive speech, and gossip.

3

Wear the approved GEMYFELL @20 anniversary attire or designated committee uniform neatly, modestly, and professionally.

How to use GEMYFELL @20

Short steps. Simple words. The app works offline, then shares your work when the internet returns.

Sign in→ Overview→ Team→ Roll Call→ + Log→ Messages→ Logs→ Sync
1
Sign inUse your phone number and PIN. First sign-in may ask you to pick a new PIN.
2
OverviewSee open cases, resolved cases, minutes, attendance rate, and recent work.
3
Team & RulesCall committee members. Secretaries can edit numbers. Read the code of conduct.
4
Roll CallPick a date. Mark each person Present, Late, Excused, or Absent. It shares with other devices. Save, then share on WhatsApp.
5
+ LogWrite an Incident Report, Meeting Minutes, or Attendance. For cases, set officer, due date, and stage.
6
MessagesFill from a saved case, choose a template, then copy or send on WhatsApp. Recent messages stay on this device.
7
Logs & PDFSearch, filter by date, export CSV, print PDF, and read the audit trail.
8
SyncWork offline anytime. Tap Refresh / Sync Cloud when online. If two phones edit the same thing, choose which copy to keep.

Simple minutes example

GEMYFELL @20 — MINUTES
Date: Sat 21 Nov 2026 | Time: 4:00-5:15 PM
Place: Church Conference Room
By: Brother Fred (Secretary)

1. Attendance
Present: Emma, Obrepong, Fred, Boahemaa, Ellen, Mark, Patrick, Gideon
Absent with apology: Esther, Janet

2. Opening
Prayer: Elder Mark
Chairman welcomed all.

3. Last minutes
Read by Fred. No changes. Adopted.

4. Decisions
- Rehearsal: come 30 minutes early. Tell your lead 2 hours before if late.
  Who: Mark and Patrick - before Wednesday rehearsal.
- Sound gear: sign out and return after each session.
  Who: Gideon - keep the log.
- Case 01: teams send figures by Tuesday 6 PM.
  Who: Boahemaa - send reminder tonight.

5. Close
Next meeting: Wed 25 Nov, 5:30 PM.
Prayer: Ellen.

Signed: Fred | Approved: Emma

Tip: write facts, decisions, and who will do what. Keep names and dates clear. Avoid blame.
