📝 Notes & Guide — Welcome to GEMYFELL @20

This is the official digital workspace for the GEMYFELL @20 Secretariat and Disciplinary Committee. It helps us keep order, accountability, and clear communication throughout the 20th-anniversary celebrations.

What This Portal Is For

Every worker and leader can see the right information at the right time: who is serving, who is present, what was agreed, and what still needs attention. The portal keeps our records in one safe place instead of scattered phone notes and messages.

Leadership→ Attendance→ Records→ Official Messages

How Each Section Works (Quick User Guide)

1
Team & RulesView committee leadership contacts, tap a number to quick-dial, and read the 5-point Volunteer Code of Conduct. Secretaries can edit phone numbers.
2
Roll CallTake fast attendance — Present, Late, Excused, or Absent — during rehearsals, meetings, and anniversary days. Choose the date first, then tap each person's status.
3
+ Log (Incidents & Minutes)Record factual incident reports, with optional compressed photo and 60-second voice notes, or take official committee meeting minutes with decisions and who will do what.
4
MessagesGenerate polite, standardized WhatsApp caution notes, committee hearing summons, or worker commendations in one tap. You can also fill a message from a saved case.
5
Logs & PDFReview past records, update case statuses from Pending to Resolved, sync across devices via Turso Cloud, or print an official PDF summary for church leadership.

For Brother Fred and the Backup Superadmin: the Admin tab manages sign-in accounts and one-time PIN resets. If anyone forgets a PIN, ask either Superadmin to reset it there.

Offline & Privacy Note

Your sign-in is PIN-protected, and each role only sees what it is allowed to see. Records are saved on this device, so the portal works 100% offline inside the auditorium when mobile signal drops. When the internet returns, changes sync automatically to the shared Turso cloud database so the Secretariat and committee see the same up-to-date information.

Tip: the Quick Start card just below is a one-page cheat sheet. Print it or send it to WhatsApp, and use the note button at the bottom-right any time for a reminder.

Worker & Volunteer Code of Conduct

1
Punctuality & Attendance:

Arrive 30 minutes before your appointed duty, sign the attendance register, and notify your Department Head at least 2 hours in advance if delayed.

2
Christian Conduct & Respect:

Treat members, guests, and fellow GEMYFELL @20 volunteers with patience and grace. Avoid public arguments, divisive speech, and gossip.

3
Dress Code & Decorum:

Wear the approved GEMYFELL @20 anniversary attire or designated committee uniform neatly, modestly, and professionally.

4
Faithful Stewardship:

Handle church equipment, audio/visual instruments, and anniversary resources carefully. Report hazards or damaged property immediately.

5
Disciplinary Escalation:

Minor infractions receive an informal verbal reminder. Repeated or serious breaches are officially referred to the Chairman & Disciplinary Panel.

Forgotten PIN — What To Do

PINs cannot be recovered from this device. For security, only a Superadmin can issue a new one-time password. There are two Superadmins so that one absent person never blocks the committee.

1
Call a SuperadminContact Brother Fred (primary) or the Backup Superadmin from the committee directory. Ask them to open Admin → Reset PIN for your phone number.
2
Get a one-time passwordThey will see it once on their screen and will give it to you privately. It expires on first use.
3
Sign in with your phone numberEnter your registered phone number and the one-time password in the sign-in box.
4
Choose a new four-digit PINThe app will ask you to create and confirm a new PIN. Keep it private and do not share it in a group chat.
5
Carry onOld sessions are signed out automatically, so only your new sign-in remains active.

If both Superadmins are unavailable, arrange a brief call with one of them — PIN resets cannot be done from the phone itself.

How to use GEMYFELL @20

Short steps. Simple words. The app works offline, then shares your work when the internet returns.

Sign in→ Overview→ Team→ Roll Call→ + Log→ Messages→ Logs→ Sync
1
Sign inUse your phone number and PIN. First sign-in may ask you to pick a new PIN.

Logs & PDFSearch, filter by date, export CSV, print PDF, and read the audit trail.
8
